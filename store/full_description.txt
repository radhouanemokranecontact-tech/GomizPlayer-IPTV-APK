GomizPlayer IPTV is a native Android TV and phone IPTV player. It plays live TV channels, movies and series from your own M3U playlists or Xtream Codes providers.

Features:
- Live TV with channel drawer, EPG Now/Next + progress, full timeline guide
- Movies & series catalogues with posters, detail modal and recommendations
- M3U/Xtream (username + password) providers; headers, custom user-agent, proxy
- EPG (XMLTV) with 26-hour window and cache
- Favourites, watch history, Continue Watching with progress
- Downloads & recordings via ffmpeg (stream copy) with progress and deletion
- Profiles (multiple user profiles with separate favourites/history)
- Player: aspect ratio, seek step, audio track cycling, screenshot, stats, subtitles settings
- Home widgets: clock, weather (Open-Meteo), prayer times (Aladhan) — Arabic/French/English
- Light & dark themes, accent color presets
- App lock (PIN), diagnostics export (ZIP), backup/restore (gomizplayer.json)
- i18n: English, Français, العربية

Requirements:
- Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer
- Android TV (Leanback) or phone
- Your own IPTV source (M3U URL or Xtream credentials)

GomizPlayer does not provide any content. You are responsible for the legality of the sources you use.
