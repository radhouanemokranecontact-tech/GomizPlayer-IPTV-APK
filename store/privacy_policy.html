GomizPlayer IPTV — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Overview

GomizPlayer IPTV is a media player for Android TV and phones. It does not collect, sell, or share personal data. It only processes data you provide (IPTV sources, favourites, history) locally on your device.

Data stored on-device

  • IPTV source URLs, usernames, passwords — stored encrypted via Android Keystore (AES-GCM)
  • Favourites, watch history, search history, profiles — stored in local app storage (gomizplayer.json)
  • App lock PIN — stored as SHA-256 hash in device-private SharedPreferences

Network requests

  • Weather (Open-Meteo), prayer times (Aladhan), geolocation (ipwho.is) — sent only if enabled in Settings; no account or advertising identifiers are sent
  • Your IPTV provider — credentials sent directly to the provider you configure; GomizPlayer never proxies or logs them

Diagnostics

The "Export diagnostics" feature creates a ZIP on-device containing app info, settings and your source list; it is shared only if you explicitly choose to share it.

Permissions

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — play IPTV streams, fetch EPG/weather/prayer
  • leanback — Android TV support

Contact

GomizPlayer IPTV is maintained as part of the GomizPlayer project. For privacy questions, contact the repository owner via the GitHub project page.
